# About
I am a Software Engineer at Fathom, based in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia. Before joining Fathom, I worked as a Senior Data Analyst at Elm. I received my BSc in Computer Science from Umm Al Qura University in 2020. I enjoy challenging problems that sit between engineering, data and visual thinking, and I'm always looking to grow my skills and build things that matter.
